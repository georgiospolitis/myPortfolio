## Composition
Every creative is one full-bleed photographic composition: no flat digital header/footer, image boxes, white margins or solid text panels. 72–88px side gutters apply to overlaid copy, not the photograph. Palette includes warm ivory/plaster/paper, cool honed grey stone, deep walnut and charcoal. Use meaningful physical metaphors, natural lighting and authentic texture. Concepts: threshold/introduction, planning proofs/services, laptop/WordPress, book-and-postcard/own presence, dark studio/selected work, folded paper/contact, envelope/welcome, connected panels/process, chairs/conversation. Vary overhead/oblique/front camera, crop, focal point, top/bottom/center/right/left typography and tonal range. Do not make nine beige walls with left-aligned copy. No gradients pretending to be photographs, icon grids, decorative blobs or service cards. Feed devices allowed ONLY in posts 03 and 05; other feed posts must contain no screens of any kind. Use genuine screenshot inserts, never generated UI or Greek copy. Refine photographs using the built-in image tool when texture interferes with copy; do not solve contrast with obvious boxes.
